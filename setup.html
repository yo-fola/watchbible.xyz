Privacy & API setup

Library, preferences and pending TXT edits stay in this browser’s local storage. No account or device sync. Use one editing tab at a time. No Apple Notes access.

Search queries, selected public catalogue IDs and chosen genre filters are sent to TMDB for matching, details and recommendations. Your raw TXT note, statuses, likes and backup files are not uploaded. Posters load from TMDB.

The token stays in server memory; no credential file is created. Keep credentials out of chat and browser code.

Streaming listings depend on region and may change. Missing data does not prove a title is unavailable. Watch options links open TMDB’s regional page, not a guessed streaming URL.
